Projects with measurable outcomes

Flagship — Samodrei LLC

AI Voice Agent Platform

An end-to-end outbound and inbound calling platform. Agents dial from a campaign list or answer a Twilio number, hold a live conversation grounded in the customer’s own documents, then classify the lead and write it back to their CRM — with no human in the loop.

  • 800–900msend-to-end round trip
  • Two-wayinbound + outbound flows
  • Cloned voiceper agent persona
  • 3 CRMsZoho · HubSpot · Salesforce

01 Conversation engine

Deepgram streaming STT with barge-in detection, adaptive-threshold VAD and noise reduction, so the agent stops mid-sentence the moment the caller speaks. GPT-4.1-mini with prompt caching writes the reply; ElevenLabs streams it back over the Twilio media stream.

02 Per-agent knowledge base

Every agent gets its own Qdrant collection built from uploaded PDFs and documents, so answers are grounded in that customer’s product data rather than the system prompt alone.

03 Campaign orchestration

An async queue with per-campaign locks, configurable retries and inter-call delays, calling-hour windows that auto-pause outside business hours, and APScheduler cron expressions for recurring runs.

04 Post-call intelligence

A summariser turns each transcript into structured JSON carrying a CRM-standard lead status, self-correcting contradictions such as a callback logged as a closed deal, and recovers email addresses spelled out letter by letter.

05 Delivery you can trust

Internal statuses map onto exact Zoho, HubSpot and Salesforce field values, sent through a webhook layer with exponential-backoff retries, a circuit breaker that trips when the CRM is down, and a dead-letter queue drained every five minutes.

06 Audit trail

Recordings and full transcripts land in S3, call metadata and campaign state in MySQL, and a millisecond-precision timing log makes every stage of the latency budget auditable after the fact.

04 — About

How I work

I’m an AI Engineer based in Faridabad, India. Most of my work sits at the point where a model stops being a notebook experiment and has to answer a real user — under a latency budget, with real data behind it, at a cost someone has to pay for.

In practice that has meant profiling every hop of a voice call to find the 100 ms hiding in a buffer, designing retrieval so an LLM cites the right paragraph instead of guessing, and putting FastAPI services on Kubernetes where they survive real traffic. I care about the unglamorous parts — observability, retries, cost per call — because they are what separates a usable AI feature from a demo.
