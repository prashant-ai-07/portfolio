02 — Projects

Projects

Selected projects, including work for ICICI Bank and Reliance General Insurance.

Samodrei LLC

AI Voice Agent Platform

An outbound and inbound AI calling platform. Agents place calls from campaign lists or answer calls to a Twilio number, hold multi-turn conversations backed by a per-agent knowledge base, then summarise the call, classify the lead and update the CRM.

  • 800–900 msround-trip voice latency
  • 3 CRMsZoho, HubSpot, Salesforce

01 Speech pipeline

Deepgram streaming speech-to-text in English and multilingual modes, with barge-in detection, voice activity detection using adaptive silence thresholds, and noise reduction before transcription. ElevenLabs turns each reply into speech in the agent’s cloned voice.

02 LLM & knowledge base

OpenAI GPT-4.1-mini with prompt caching generates replies. Each agent has its own Qdrant knowledge base built from uploaded PDFs and documents, queried when the caller asks a specific question. Conversation history is kept per call, with anti-repetition logic.

03 Campaign orchestration

Async queue-based bulk calling with per-campaign locks; configurable retries, retry delay and inter-call delay; calling-hour windows per user or contact list; APScheduler cron schedules for recurring campaigns; campaign progress tracked in MySQL.

04 Post-call intelligence

After every call, GPT parses the transcript into structured JSON with a CRM lead status such as OPEN_DEAL or BAD_TIMING. Inconsistent statuses are auto-corrected, and email addresses spelled out letter by letter are extracted.

05 CRM & calendar integration

Lead statuses are mapped to Zoho, HubSpot and Salesforce field values. Webhooks retry with exponential backoff (up to 3 attempts), a circuit breaker pauses delivery while a CRM is down, and a dead-letter queue retries failures every 5 minutes. Google Calendar integration stores meeting event IDs and Meet links.

06 Storage & logging

Call recordings (MP3, with WAV fallback) and transcripts are stored in AWS S3; transcripts and call metadata in MySQL. A millisecond-precision timing log records latency for auditing.
